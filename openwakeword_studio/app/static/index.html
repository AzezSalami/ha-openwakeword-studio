Use your wake words in Home Assistant

  1. Add the integration. Go to Settings → Devices & services. Home Assistant should show a discovered Wyoming Protocol entry called “openWakeWord Studio” — press Add.
    Not discovered? Choose Add integration → Wyoming Protocol and enter host … and port 10400.
  2. Pick a wake word. Open Settings → Voice assistants, edit your assistant, and under Wake word choose openwakeword as engine and one of your enabled wake words.
  3. Talk to a device. Any voice satellite that streams audio to Home Assistant for wake word detection will now use this add-on. Watch it happen on the Overview tab.

Tuning tips

  • Use the Test tab: say the wake word a few times and note the peak score, then say other things. Put the threshold between the two.
  • False activations? Raise the threshold or trigger level, or turn on the voice activity filter.
  • Missed activations? Lower the threshold for that wake word only (on its card, “Custom sensitivity”).
  • The microphone test needs Home Assistant to be opened over https://. Audio file tests work everywhere.

Custom wake words

Train your own model with the openWakeWord training notebook, or pick one from the community, then upload the .tflite or .onnx file on the Wake words tab. Files are stored in /share/openwakeword; models you copy there yourself show up after Rescan folder.

After adding or enabling a wake word, reload the Wyoming integration in Home Assistant so it appears in the list.
